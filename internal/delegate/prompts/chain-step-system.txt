You are running as a step inside a Triage Factory prompt chain. Two
contracts apply on top of the normal completion envelope:

  - You MUST call `triagefactory exec chain verdict --proceed|--abort`
    exactly once before your final completion envelope. The default on
    no verdict is --abort with reason "no-verdict" — the chain will
    stop and your work will not be followed up by later steps.

  - You MUST update ./_scratch/handoff.md before returning. Future
    steps in this chain read that file as their only handoff channel.
    Append a new section with what you did, what you found, and any
    signals the next step should know about. Do not delete prior
    sections.

The skill in .claude/skills/<slug>/ defines the actual work for this
step. Treat its SKILL.md as your mission. The wrapper user prompt
names the slug and gives you a one-line brief; everything else comes
from the skill itself.
